Burst pipe repair in Mundelein, IL starts at the shutoff

A burst pipe is a split or break in a supply line that releases water under pressure. In Mundelein the usual causes are winter freezing, corroded older pipe and failed fittings. Even a small split can release hundreds of gallons in an hour.

Our 24/7 line dispatches a licensed plumber right away. We stop the leak, cut out the damaged section, replace it and test the line. When the break reveals a bigger pipe problem, we explain what we found so you can plan.

Do these things before we arrive

Close the main water shutoff, usually near the water meter in the basement. Open a faucet at the lowest level to drain the line. Switch off electricity to wet rooms at the breaker if you can reach it safely, and keep out of standing water if outlets are low. Move belongings, take photos for your insurer and keep the water heater off if the cold water is shut off.

Causes of a burst pipe

  • Water freezing inside a pipe in an unheated wall, garage or crawl space.
  • Corrosion on old galvanized or copper, ending in a pinhole that tears.
  • Excess water pressure from a failed pressure regulator.
  • Failed solder or crimp joints and push fittings.
  • A nail or screw driven into a line during remodeling.

Winter and the Mundelein freeze

Lake County winters bring long stretches below freezing. Pipes in exterior walls, garages and unheated crawl spaces are the first to freeze. If a pipe has frozen but not yet split, open the nearest tap, apply gentle heat with a hair dryer, never an open flame, and call us if you cannot reach the frozen section.

Pipes can split at the ice plug and show leaks only after the thaw. If you find a wet ceiling after a cold snap, treat it as a burst. Our leak detection service pinpoints hidden splits, and repiping is the permanent answer when old lines fail repeatedly.

What affects burst pipe repair pricing

Cost depends on access and on what failed.

  • Pipe location. An exposed basement line is faster than one in a finished wall.
  • Material. Copper, PEX and galvanized pipe have different repair methods.
  • Extent. A single split differs from a run of several.
  • Water cleanup. We repair the pipe. Water removal and drying are separate trades.
  • Hour. After-hours dispatch is priced separately.

After the repair: drying and records

Once the pipe is fixed, the wet area still needs to dry. Run fans, open cabinet doors and keep the heat on so moisture leaves the wall cavity. Photograph the damage and keep the plumber's written description of the failure.

That paperwork helps if you file a claim. Ask your insurance agent what they need before you throw anything away.

Code for pipe repair

Replacing a burst section uses approved pipe and fittings under the Illinois Plumbing Code, the state standard (Title 77, Part 890 of the Illinois Administrative Code). A short repair is often quick, but a long section or a main line may need a permit. Ask before you hire anyone who offers to skip it.
