Boiler repair in Mundelein, IL when the heat stops

A boiler heats water and circulates it through radiators or baseboard pipes. When it fails you may have no heat, loud banging, low pressure, leaks at the unit or no hot water if the boiler also supplies it. Boiler repair finds the failed part and replaces it.

Our 24/7 line sends a licensed plumber. Because winter temperatures in Lake County drop fast, we treat a no-heat boiler call as urgent. We stabilize the system first and explain what is needed for a lasting fix.

No heat tonight: what to do first

If you smell gas, leave the building and call the gas utility or 911 before you call us. Install a carbon monoxide alarm near sleeping areas. If the boiler is leaking, shut off its water feed and power, and do not remove covers. If pipes may freeze, open cabinet doors under sinks and keep a faucet dripping while you wait.

Boiler problems we see

  • No heat, with the boiler not firing or the thermostat not calling.
  • A pressure gauge that reads too low or climbs to the relief setting.
  • A leak from the relief valve, a fitting or the boiler body.
  • Banging, ticking or gurgling in the pipes, which can point to air or low water.
  • A circulator pump that hums but does not move water.
  • Zones that are cold while others are warm, often a zone valve or air in the loop.

What we handle and what we hand off

We handle the plumbing side of boilers: leaks, relief valves, expansion tanks, fill valves, circulator pumps, zone valves and piping. For specialized burner or combustion service, we advise when a heating contractor is the better call. We tell you early so you are not paying for the wrong visit.

If your boiler also heats the domestic hot water, a failure can look like a water heater problem. For a boiler that is beyond repair, our plumbing installation team can plan the piping for a replacement.

What affects boiler repair pricing

Boiler work varies, and we quote before starting.

  • Part. A pump or valve costs less than a control board.
  • System type. Steam, hot water and combi systems differ.
  • Access. Tight mechanical rooms take longer.
  • Age. Older units may need parts that are hard to find.
  • Hour. No-heat dispatch at night is priced separately.

If the boiler is near the end of its life, we will say so rather than sell repeated repairs.

Older Mundelein homes and hydronic heat

Some older Mundelein houses, including homes near downtown and the Metra station, use hot water or steam radiators fed by a boiler in the basement. These systems are durable but have many valves, vents and fittings that can leak. Keep the area around the boiler clear and note the pressure gauge reading when it is working normally.

Standards for boiler work

Gas boilers are serviced to the manufacturer's instructions and the benchmark set by NFPA 54, the National Fuel Gas Code. Water side and relief valve work fall under the Illinois Plumbing Code, the state standard (Title 77, Part 890 of the Illinois Administrative Code). A boiler with a failed safety control stays off until the control is repaired.
