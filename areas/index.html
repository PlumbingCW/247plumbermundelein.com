Inside Mundelein

Within the village we serve downtown Mundelein and the Metra station area, the Route 45 and Lake Street corridor, the Route 60 and Route 83 subdivisions, the University of Saint Mary of the Lake campus area, Diamond Lake and Countryside Lake. Older homes near downtown tend to have different pipe materials than newer subdivisions, and we plan the visit with that in mind.

Mundelein Public Works at 847-949-3270 handles street-side water and sewer questions, and the building office at 847-949-3283 handles permits.

Why local matters in plumbing

Water hardness, soil, groundwater and housing age all change what goes wrong. Lake Michigan water arrives in Mundelein at about 8 grains per gallon according to the Central Lake County Joint Action Water Agency. Parts of Lake Zurich and Hawthorn Woods use wells or a mix of supplies, and those homes have pressure tanks and other equipment to consider.

That is why the first question on every call is about your property and your system, not just your problem.
