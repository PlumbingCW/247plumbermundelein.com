Sewer line cleaning in Mundelein, IL for a blocked main

Sewer line cleaning removes roots, grease and debris from the main drain that leaves your house. A blocked main shows up as gurgling toilets, a floor drain that fills when the washing machine runs, or several fixtures backing up together.

We dispatch around the clock. A licensed plumber opens the cleanout, runs a cable with the right cutting head and flushes the line. When the blockage returns often we suggest a camera look to learn why.

While you wait for the plumber

Do not flush toilets or run the dishwasher and washing machine. Every gallon goes to the lowest drain. If your cleanout is outside and you can reach it, leave the cap on until we arrive, because a pressurized line can spray. Keep people away from the basement if sewage is on the floor.

Common causes of a blocked main

  • Roots entering at a joint and growing across the pipe.
  • Grease from the kitchen cooling and building up on the wall.
  • Wipes, hygiene products and paper towels that do not break down.
  • A belly in the pipe where solids settle.
  • A broken or collapsed section that traps debris.

Cable first, jet when needed

A cutting cable clears most main-line clogs quickly and is often the right first step. When the cable only opens a small hole or the line fills again within weeks, we recommend hydro jetting, which scours the pipe wall.

We never promise that a cable will cure a line that is physically damaged. If the pipe is cracked, collapsed or offset, cleaning only buys time. In that case we show the camera footage and move to sewer line repair so you stop paying for temporary fixes.

What affects sewer cleaning pricing

Sewer cleaning is priced by line and method, and you hear the number first.

  • Access. An exterior cleanout is faster than removing a toilet.
  • Length. Longer runs to the street take more cable and time.
  • Condition. Heavy roots may need multiple passes.
  • Add-ons. Camera work before and after is priced separately.
  • Hour. After-hours dispatch uses a different rate.

What homeowners near the lakes and old downtown should know

Lots in the Diamond Lake and Countryside Lake areas can have high water tables, which saturate the soil around a sewer lateral and push roots toward moisture. Houses near downtown Mundelein often have older laterals made of clay tile with many joints, and each joint is an entry point. A yearly cleaning is cheaper than an emergency call.

If you want to see why a line keeps clogging, book a sewer camera inspection after the cleaning.

Standards for line cleaning

Cleaning is maintenance and normally needs no permit. Any repair that follows is covered by the Illinois Plumbing Code, the state standard (Title 77, Part 890 of the Illinois Administrative Code). Mundelein Public Works at 847-949-3270 can confirm whether a blockage sits in the village main or your lateral.

What to do before we arrive at a sewer backup

Treat any backup as a health matter.

  • Stop all water use in the house.
  • Keep people and pets away from affected floors and fixtures.
  • Ventilate if the smell is strong, and avoid touching surfaces without gloves.
  • Do not use chemical drain cleaners in a backed-up main line.
  • Call (847) 281-8626 and ask for the emergency dispatcher.
