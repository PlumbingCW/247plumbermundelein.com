Faucet repair in Mundelein, IL for drips and bursts

Faucet and sink repair covers dripping spouts, leaking handles, failed cartridges, broken shutoff valves and supply lines. A drip wastes water slowly. A failed supply line or a valve that will not close can flood a kitchen within minutes.

Our 24/7 line sends a licensed plumber who shuts off the water first, then repairs or replaces the part that failed. Many fixes take one visit, and we carry common cartridges, supply lines and valves.

Water spraying or a valve that will not close

Close the angle stop under the sink by turning it clockwise. If it will not move, go to the main shutoff, usually near the water meter, and turn it off. Do not force a corroded valve with a wrench, because the stem can snap. Towels around the cabinet buy time while you call the 24/7 line.

Fixture problems we repair

  • A faucet that drips from the spout or leaks at the handle.
  • A shutoff valve that is stuck, seeping or has a broken handle.
  • A braided supply line with a bulge, rust or a slow leak.
  • A single-handle faucet with weak flow, often from a clogged aerator or a worn cartridge.
  • A bathtub or shower valve that drips or will not shut off fully.
  • Sink drains and traps that leak under the cabinet.

Shower and tub valves, plus kitchen faucets

Shower and tub valves sit behind the wall, so a drip may mean replacing a cartridge or an entire valve body. We open the access panel when one exists and plan the smallest cut in the wall when it does not. A tub spout that leaks after the shower is turned off is often the diverter, not the valve.

Kitchen faucets with sprayers fail at the hose. We replace the hose and check the connections to the supply. If you also have a slow sink, see drain cleaning. A garbage disposal that leaks at the sink flange is covered on garbage disposal repair.

What affects faucet repair pricing

Most faucet jobs are modest, and we say the price before starting.

  • Part cost. Cartridges and valves vary by brand.
  • Wall access. Shower valves need a cut in the wall or an access panel.
  • Fixture supplied. A homeowner-supplied faucet changes the materials cost.
  • Age of shutoffs. Old stops often fail when disturbed, so we replace them.
  • Hour. After-hours calls have their own rate.

Lake Michigan water in Mundelein carries moderate hardness, so aerators and cartridges can scale over time. A vinegar soak helps between replacements.

Aerators and flow

A faucet that sprays sideways or runs weakly often has a clogged aerator. Unscrew the tip, rinse out grit and soak it in vinegar for scale. If the flow stays weak, the problem is behind the aerator in the cartridge or supply line.

Standards for fixtures

Fixture repairs rarely need a permit, but replacing shutoff valves, supply lines or drain parts should meet the Illinois Plumbing Code, the state standard (Title 77, Part 890 of the Illinois Administrative Code). We use parts rated for potable water and tell you when a repair crosses into permitted work.

What to do before we arrive at a faucet leak

A few quick steps limit the damage.

  • Close the shutoff valves under the sink by turning them clockwise.
  • If a valve will not close, shut the main water valve.
  • Place a bucket and towels under the leak and clear the cabinet.
  • Check the cabinet floor for soft wood or swelling, and photograph it.
  • Call (847) 281-8626 if the water will not stop.
