Hydro jetting for Mundelein drains and sewer lines

Hydro jetting sends high-pressure water through a hose and a nozzle that fires jets forward and backward inside the pipe. The water cuts roots, strips grease and scale from the wall and flushes the debris out through the cleanout. Cabling punches through a clog. Jetting scours the whole bore.

Our 24/7 dispatch handles emergency jetting calls when a main line is backing up into bathrooms or a floor drain. We camera the line before and after when possible, so you see what was cleared.

When jetting beats cabling

  • A cable stuck or snagged at a particular distance, such as the first bend.
  • The same line clogging every few weeks or months.
  • Heavy grease from a kitchen or commercial line.
  • Root fibers entering at joints, which return unless the wall is scoured.
  • Scale build-up inside older cast iron lines that narrows the pipe.

Safe steps while a backup is active

Stop using water in the house, including toilets and washers. Keep people out of any flooded basement area until you know the electrical panel is dry. Do not pour chemicals into a backed-up line, because they can splash back onto the plumber and the fixtures. Then call the 24/7 line.

What drives jetting prices

Jetting is priced by the line, not a flat rate.

  • Pipe length and size. A long four-inch main takes longer than a short branch.
  • Condition. Heavy roots or scale need more passes.
  • Access. A good cleanout speeds the job, while a toilet pull adds time.
  • Camera work. Before and after inspection is an add-on with its own cost.
  • Hour of dispatch. After-hours calls carry a different rate.

If the camera finds a broken pipe, jetting would only wash more soil into the line, so we stop and discuss sewer line repair instead.

Why Mundelein lines clog from roots and age

Mature trees across Mundelein neighborhoods put pressure on older clay and cast iron lines. Roots find hairline cracks, enter at joints and thicken into mats that catch paper and grease. A cable opens the line for a short time, then the mat grows back.

Jetting also suits restaurant and shop lines near Route 45 and Route 60, where grease accumulates on the wall. For a quick drain clog start with drain cleaning. If you see repeated backups, schedule jetting and a camera visit so the pipe is checked.

Rules that apply to jetting

Jetting clears pipe and does not change it, so it usually needs no permit. If the camera shows a defect that needs repair, that repair falls under the Illinois Plumbing Code, the state standard (Title 77, Part 890 of the Illinois Administrative Code). We tell you which part of a job is cleaning and which part is repair.

What to do before we arrive at a backup

A backup carries bacteria, so protect your family first.

  • Stop using sinks, tubs, washers and toilets so no more water enters the line.
  • Keep children and pets out of the wet area, and wear gloves if you must touch anything.
  • Move belongings off the floor and photograph the damage.
  • Do not mix chemical cleaners, which can release dangerous fumes.
  • Call (847) 281-8626 and tell the dispatcher which drains are affected.
