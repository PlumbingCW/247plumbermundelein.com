Housing age and the pipes behind the walls

Libertyville has a mix of older streets near the downtown and newer subdivisions toward the edges. If your house predates the 1960s, there is a fair chance it still has some galvanized supply or cast iron drains somewhere. Those materials scale inside and fail at joints. Newer houses more often run into PEX fitting leaks and grease clogs.

That is why a plumber starts by asking when the house was built. It changes how we look for hidden leaks and which drain tools we carry. For a floor drain that gurgles, we start with drain cleaning and move to a camera if the clog returns.
