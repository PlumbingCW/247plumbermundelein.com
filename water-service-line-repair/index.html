Water service line repair in Mundelein, IL between the meter and the house

The water service line is the buried pipe that carries water from the village main and meter to your house. When it leaks, you may see a wet or sunken patch in the yard, hear no water at all, or watch the meter spin with the house shut off.

Our 24/7 line sends a licensed plumber to confirm the leak, locate it and explain repair or replacement. Because the line is buried, we use detection first and dig second.

No water or a wet yard: what to do first

Turn off the main valve inside the house, then check the meter. If the dial still moves, water is escaping on the buried line. If the meter stops, the leak is inside. Mundelein Public Works can help with questions about the meter and village main at 847-949-3270. Take a photo of the meter reading and the time.

Signs the service line is failing

  • Low pressure everywhere in the house with the meter running.
  • A wet, soft or unusually green strip of lawn between the street and the house.
  • Water pooling near the foundation wall where the line enters.
  • A hissing sound near the meter or the basement entry point.
  • Dirty water after a repair or a main break, which can indicate a damaged line.

Water source and what it means for your line

Mundelein is served with Lake Michigan water through the Central Lake County Joint Action Water Agency. The Countryside Lake system is run separately by Lake County Public Works, which publishes its own annual water report, so check which provider serves your address before you call for a meter issue.

If you want to replace old pipe inside as well, the repiping page covers the whole house. If the leak turns out to be inside, we move to leak detection and repair.

What affects water line pricing

Digging is the big variable, and we price it before starting.

  • Length and depth. Longer, deeper lines cost more.
  • Surface. Lawn, driveway and sidewalk all change restoration.
  • Method. Open trench or boring differs in time and cost.
  • Permits. Ask the Village of Mundelein building office at 847-949-3283.
  • Utility locates. Marking buried utilities is required before digging.

Who is responsible for which section

In general, the homeowner is responsible for the water service line on the property side of the meter or the curb stop, and the utility is responsible for the main in the street. The exact dividing point varies, so confirm with Mundelein Public Works at 847-949-3270 before you dig.

Call before any excavation. Utility locators mark buried lines, and hitting an unmarked line is costly and dangerous.

Rules for service line work

Service line work follows the Illinois Plumbing Code, the state standard (Title 77, Part 890 of the Illinois Administrative Code) and the village's own rules for excavation and meter connections. Mundelein Public Works at 847-949-3270 can explain where the village's responsibility ends. Call before any digging.
