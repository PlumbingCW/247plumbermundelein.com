Water heater replacement in Mundelein, IL when the tank gives out

Water heater replacement is the removal of a failed or failing heater and the installation of a new one that matches your household demand. In Mundelein the typical emergency starts with a puddle in the basement or a heater that no longer makes hot water at all.

Our dispatch line answers 24/7. A licensed plumber confirms the fuel type, tank size and venting, shuts off the water and gas or power, and tells you your replacement options before any work starts. Many replacements are completed the same day when the right unit is available.

If the tank is leaking right now

Close the cold water supply valve on top of the heater and switch the power off at the breaker, or turn the gas control to off. Open a hot tap upstairs to relieve pressure and move belongings away from the water. If the floor drain is clogged, tell us when you call, because a full tank can release around forty gallons into the basement.

Signs the heater should be replaced rather than repaired

  • Water at the base of the tank that does not come from a fitting.
  • A heater beyond roughly ten years that has needed more than one repair.
  • Rust-colored hot water that does not clear after flushing the tank.
  • Loud popping from heavy sediment, together with a drop in capacity.
  • Rising gas or electric bills with no change in hot water use.

What affects the replacement price

Price is set by the unit and the conditions, and you see it before work begins.

  • Size and type. A 40-gallon standard tank and a tankless unit sit far apart in price.
  • Venting. A new liner or a vent change adds parts and labor.
  • Code upgrades. Expansion tanks, drain pans or corrected gas piping may be required.
  • Access. Stairs, tight closets and long runs affect time.
  • Timing. Emergency dispatch outside business hours is priced separately.

Sizing a heater for a Mundelein household

Size follows household use. Two people with one bathroom need less than a family of five that runs showers, a dishwasher and laundry back to back. We ask about peak hours, because a tank that is too small runs out and one that is too large wastes fuel.

Many houses between Route 45 and the lakes of Diamond Lake and Countryside Lake have basement utility rooms, which makes a straightforward tank swap easy. If your water heater sits in a closet on the main floor, tell us in advance so we can plan for a drain pan and a safe path for old-tank removal.

Hardness from the Lake Michigan supply is moderate, so we also recommend an anode rod check and a yearly flush on any new tank. If you also need drain work, see drain cleaning.

Attached homes need shutoffs and drain pans planned in, as described for Cambridge Country townhomes and houses.

Code and permit notes for replacement

Replacing a water heater is covered by the Illinois Plumbing Code, the state standard (Title 77, Part 890 of the Illinois Administrative Code), the manufacturer's installation manual and, for gas units, the benchmark set by NFPA 54, the National Fuel Gas Code. The Mundelein building office at 847-949-3283 can tell you whether a permit applies.
