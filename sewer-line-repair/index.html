Sewer line repair in Mundelein, IL after a backup

Sewer line repair restores the pipe that carries wastewater from your house to the village main. When that pipe cracks, collapses, shifts at a joint or fills with roots, wastewater backs up into the lowest drains. That is why sewer failures show up first in basement floor drains and first-floor toilets.

Our 24/7 line dispatches a licensed plumber for sewer emergencies. The first job is to stop the backup. The second is to find out why it happened, so the fix lasts.

Sewage in the house: what to do first

Stop all water use, including toilets, sinks and laundry. Keep children and pets out of the affected area, because sewage carries bacteria. Do not run the furnace or touch outlets near standing water, and do not wade in water if electrical panels are low. Call our 24/7 line. We can pump, clear and then inspect the line.

What breaks a sewer line

  • Tree roots that enter at joints and then thicken into a mat.
  • Cast iron or clay pipe that has scaled, cracked or sagged with age.
  • A belly or sag where soil settled, so water and solids pool.
  • Offset joints after ground movement or old construction.
  • A collapsed section, usually found by camera after repeated clogs.

Finding the problem before choosing the fix

A camera runs through the line and records the exact location and depth of the damage. We mark the spot on the surface and decide whether the pipe needs a small spot repair or a longer replacement. If the line is only clogged, our sewer line cleaning page covers clearing it without repairs.

Cameras also avoid guesswork on price. A five-foot repair under a lawn is a very different job from replacing a line under a driveway, a porch or a slab.

What drives sewer repair pricing

Sewer jobs run from a short spot fix to a long replacement, and the price follows scope.

  • Length and depth. Longer and deeper lines require more work.
  • Surface above. Lawn, concrete, landscaping and driveways change the restoration.
  • Method. Lining, bursting and excavation have different costs.
  • Permits and inspection. Some repairs require a permit. Ask the Village of Mundelein building office at 847-949-3283.
  • Hour of the call. Emergency dispatch is priced separately.

Sewer backup coverage is often a separate endorsement on a homeowners policy. Check yours before filing a claim.

Permits and the code for sewer work

A sewer repair or replacement falls under the Illinois Plumbing Code, the state standard (Title 77, Part 890 of the Illinois Administrative Code), and most villages require a permit and an inspection of the new pipe before it is covered. The Mundelein building office at 847-949-3283 handles that for properties in the village.
