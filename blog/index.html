When to call a plumber instead of reading

Guides help, but some situations need a plumber now.

  • Water that will not stop is an emergency, so close the main valve and call.
  • Sewage in the house should be treated as a health hazard.
  • A smell of gas means leave the house and call the gas utility from outside.
  • Any leak near electrical outlets calls for switching off power from a dry location.

Where the guidance comes from

Technical points in these guides follow the Illinois Plumbing Code, the state standard administered by the Illinois Department of Public Health, and the manufacturer's instructions. Local facts come from agencies such as the Central Lake County Joint Action Water Agency and the village offices.
