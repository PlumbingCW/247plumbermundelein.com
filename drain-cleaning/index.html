Drain cleaning in Mundelein, IL starts with finding the clog

Drain cleaning is the removal of a blockage from a sink, tub, floor drain or sewer line so water flows freely again. In Mundelein the call usually starts the same way. One fixture drains slowly, then a toilet gurgles, then water sits in the tub.

Our dispatch line is open 24/7. A licensed plumber asks which drains are affected, then explains if the problem is a single fixture trap or a blockage in the main line. That one question decides the tool, the time and the price.

You do not need to know the cause before you call. Describe what the water is doing and we will work out the rest on site.

Drain backing up: what to do first

Stop running water in the house the moment a drain backs up. Every flush and every load of laundry sends more water toward the blockage. If the toilet is overflowing, close the valve behind it and lift the tank lid float to stop refilling.

Keep chemical drain openers out of the line. They sit in standing water, burn skin and can damage old pipe, and they make the next plumber's job harder. Call our 24/7 line instead and keep children and pets away from the wet floor.

Signs a clog is bigger than one fixture

Watch for these patterns, which point to the main drain rather than a single trap.

  • More than one drain is slow at the same time, especially the lowest tub or floor drain in the basement.
  • A toilet flush makes the shower or sink gurgle, because air is being pushed back through the vent system.
  • Water rises in a basement floor drain after the washing machine drains.
  • The same drain clears for a week and then clogs again, which points to roots, grease or a belly in the pipe.
  • A sour sewer smell lingers near the floor drain even when the trap is full of water.

Kitchen sinks, bathroom drains and the main line

Kitchen sink clogs are usually grease, soap and food residue packed into the trap arm and the horizontal run behind the wall. We cable the line, then flush it with hot water to carry the loosened film out. Bathroom sinks and tubs more often fill with hair and soap scum near the stopper.

A main line clog affects every drain below the blockage. We run a cable with a cutting head from the cleanout, and when the line has roots or heavy buildup we recommend hydro jetting because a cable only punches a hole through the debris.

If a cable keeps hitting the same obstruction at the same distance, we offer a camera look through sewer camera inspection so you can see the pipe before choosing a repair.

What affects the price of drain cleaning

We quote the job before work begins, and the number depends on a handful of real factors.

  • Which line is blocked. A bathroom sink costs less to clear than a main sewer line.
  • Access. A cleanout outside the house is quicker than pulling a toilet to reach the line.
  • Method. Cabling, jetting and camera work are priced differently.
  • Time of day. After-hours dispatch carries a different rate than a weekday visit.
  • Cause. Roots or a damaged pipe may need follow-up work beyond the clog.

Homeowners insurance usually covers sudden water damage, but a gradual drain backup is often excluded unless you carry a sewer backup endorsement. Check your policy before a claim.

Mundelein homes, older pipe and where the clogs form

Mundelein has homes from several building eras, from older houses near downtown and the Metra station to newer subdivisions along Route 60 and Route 83. Houses built before the 1960s often have cast iron or clay drain lines that scale inside and catch roots at the joints. Newer plastic lines clog from grease and wipes more than from pipe damage.

Lakeside areas such as Diamond Lake and Countryside Lake add a second factor, because some lots sit low and drains near the basement slope toward a sump. If your floor drain backs up during heavy rain, tell the dispatcher, because that can point to the village sewer or storm flow rather than a clog in your own line. Village Public Works at 847-949-3270 can tell you whether the street main is affected.

Codes behind drain work

Drain, waste and vent work is governed by the Illinois Plumbing Code, the state standard (Title 77, Part 890 of the Illinois Administrative Code). Clearing a clog does not need a permit, but replacing a trap, a stack or a section of drain often does. The Mundelein building office at 847-949-3283 can say where the line is drawn for your address.
