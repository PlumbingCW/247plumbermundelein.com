Commercial plumbing in Mundelein, IL, day or night

Commercial plumbing covers the pipes, drains, heaters and fixtures in buildings that serve customers or tenants. A restaurant with a clogged grease line, a clinic with a leaking supply or a landlord with a tenant calling at midnight all need a plumber who answers quickly.

Our 24/7 line is staffed for these calls. A licensed plumber arrives with the equipment for the building type, stops the damage and then talks through a lasting fix. We also schedule planned work outside business hours when you cannot close.

Active leak or backup at your business

Shut off the main water valve or the zone valve, switch off equipment in wet areas and move inventory off the floor. Post a sign on any wet walkway. If a grease trap is overflowing, stop using the kitchen sinks. Then call the 24/7 line and tell us the type of building so we send the right tools.

Typical commercial calls

  • Kitchen drain lines and grease buildup that slow the line.
  • Main sewer backups that flood a floor drain after hours.
  • Water heater failures in offices, salons and clinics.
  • Restroom fixture repair, including flush valves and urinals.
  • Leaks in ceilings that threaten electrical equipment.
  • Work orders from property managers on behalf of tenants.

Property managers and tenant calls

If you manage rental units or a retail strip, you can call on a tenant's behalf. Tell us the address, the contact who can let us in and what the tenant reported. We keep notes and photos for your work order file. Property managers need documentation, and we provide it.

Businesses along Route 45, Route 60 and Route 83 vary from restaurants to medical offices, and each has different drain and water demands. See our drain cleaning page for the first response to backups and the leak detection page for hidden water.

What affects commercial pricing

Commercial work is quoted before it starts, and the number depends on these factors.

  • Building and fixture count. More fixtures and longer lines raise time.
  • Hour. Nights, weekends and holidays have different rates.
  • Equipment. Jetters, cameras and pumps are billed with the job.
  • Scope. A one-time fix versus a scheduled maintenance plan.
  • Code or permit needs. Some repairs need village approval. Ask the Village of Mundelein building office at 847-949-3283.

Large or multi-day commercial jobs are quoted in writing before work starts, so your manager or owner can approve the scope.

Planning work around business hours

A shop, office or restaurant cannot always close for a repair. We plan around your hours, shut off only the section that has to be isolated and tell you how long water will be down. After-hours work is common for kitchens and retail sites.

Give the dispatcher a contact who can approve work and open the site, and tell us about alarms, lease rules and any landlord approvals before we arrive.

Code for commercial work

Commercial plumbing falls under the Illinois Plumbing Code, the state standard (Title 77, Part 890 of the Illinois Administrative Code) and often under additional village and health rules for food service and public restrooms. Grease interceptors, backflow devices and fixture counts are common review items.
