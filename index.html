Drains and sewer

Backups, clogs and sewer lines

A single slow sink is a nuisance. Several drains slowing at once, a gurgling toilet or water in the basement floor drain points to the main line. We clear it, then run a camera so you can see what caused it.

Older homes near downtown Mundelein and the Metra station often have clay or cast iron laterals that roots and scale narrow. Newer subdivisions off Route 60 and Route 83 have smoother pipe, but grease and low spots still cause trouble.

Start with drain cleaning, then ask about a sewer camera inspection if backups repeat.

Water heaters

No hot water, a leaking tank or an upgrade

A water heater that fails at night leaves the family without hot water and sometimes with a flooded floor. We repair what is repairable and replace what is not. Tank or tankless, the plumber checks venting, gas supply and sizing before quoting.

Mundelein water is moderately hard at about 8 grains per gallon, per the Central Lake County Joint Action Water Agency. Scale builds slowly, and a yearly flush helps any heater last longer.

Leaks and pipes

Hidden leaks, burst pipes and old supply lines

A rising water bill, a warm spot on the floor or the sound of running water with everything off can mean a hidden leak. We locate it with listening gear and pressure tests, then open the smallest section needed.

Houses built before the 1970s may still carry galvanized or early copper supply lines. After several leaks, repiping often costs less than patching again. A frozen or burst pipe is an emergency, and burst pipe repair starts with the main valve.

Local knowledge

Plumbing that fits Mundelein homes

Mundelein runs from older blocks around downtown and the Metra North Central Service station to newer subdivisions along Route 60 and Route 83. It includes the lake communities at Diamond Lake and Countryside Lake and the campus area around the University of Saint Mary of the Lake. Housing age and groundwater change what fails and how we fix it.

Lake County has areas of high groundwater, so sump pumps and floor drains matter in low lots near the lakes. Our work follows the Illinois Plumbing Code, the state standard administered by the Illinois Department of Public Health. For permits, the Mundelein building office is at 847-949-3283. For street-side water and sewer, Mundelein Public Works is at 847-949-3270.
