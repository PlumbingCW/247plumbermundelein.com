The center of the village

Mundelein was incorporated in 1909 under an earlier name, Rockefeller, and the village hall now sits next to the Metra station in the center of town. The station is on Metra's North Central Service,.

Neighborhood data for the Mundelein Station area shows a mix of mid-century homes and newer homes from the 1990s, with a median build year of 1962. Plumbing in that kind of mix is varied, so each address needs its own look.
