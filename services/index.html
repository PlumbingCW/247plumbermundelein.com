How a service call works

You call, we ask your street, the fixture and what the water is doing. We give you steps to limit damage, such as closing the main valve, and a plumber is assigned. On arrival the plumber finds the cause, explains the options and gives the price before starting work.

For non-emergency work we schedule a visit that fits your day. For emergencies, the line is answered at any hour including nights, weekends and holidays.

Licensing and permits

Illinois licenses plumbers through the Illinois Department of Public Health, and you can verify any plumber on the state lookup tool before you hire. Work follows the Illinois Plumbing Code, the state standard. Many jobs, such as water heater replacement, sewer repair and repiping, need a permit. The Mundelein building office at 847-949-3283 can tell you what applies to your address.

We explain what permits a job needs and make sure the work meets the local rules before we call it complete.

Safety steps while you wait for a plumber

These basic steps apply to most emergencies.

  • Close the main water valve for any active leak.
  • Switch off power to wet areas from a dry panel.
  • Stop using drains during a backup.
  • If you smell gas, leave the house and call the gas utility from outside.
