A lake community of mid-century homes

Loch Lomond lies in Mundelein around Loch Lomond Lake, reached from Dunleer off Dunbar Road and Route 45. Subdivision data lists homes built between 1950 and 1985, and neighborhood data shows a median build year of 1962, mostly ranch and split-level designs.

One listing source notes that the lake was once privately owned and that Roscoe Homes began building U.S. Steel homes on the site in the mid-1950s. Homes from that period often have been remodeled several times, so the plumbing inside a given house can be a mix of old and new.

Do not assume what is behind your walls

House age alone does not tell us what pipe is inside. Previous owners may have replaced supply lines, water heaters or drains. We identify the material at your house by looking, not by assuming, and we tell you what we find before we suggest anything.

What we look at near the lake

Lake-oriented lots bring a few checks that inland streets do not need.

  • Sump pump condition, float switch, check valve and discharge direction.
  • Battery backup so the pump keeps running during a power outage.
  • Floor drains in lower levels and whether they back up after heavy rain.
  • Water heater age and drain pan in finished lower levels.
  • Sewer lateral condition if drains slow on more than one fixture.
