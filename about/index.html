About Mundelein 24/7 Plumbing

We are a Mundelein-based plumbing service built around one idea: when water is the problem, you should reach a person right away.

Call (847) 281-8626See services

How we work

Mundelein 24/7 Plumbing answers its line around the clock and sends licensed plumbers to homes and small businesses in Mundelein and the neighboring towns. We keep the process simple. You call, we ask what you see and a plumber is assigned.

We do not hide behind forms. There is no online request box to wait on. The phone is the fastest way to stop a leak, and it is how we work.

What you can expect

Before work starts, the plumber explains what is wrong, what the options are and what each costs. If a repair does not make sense, we say so. If a permit is needed, we tell you which office to call. Calls to our line may be recorded for quality and training purposes.

Licensing is a matter of public record. Illinois licenses plumbers through the Illinois Department of Public Health, and you can look up any plumber with the state tool before you hire.

Our local focus

We know Mundelein's older blocks near downtown and the Metra station, the newer subdivisions off Route 60 and Route 83, the lake communities at Diamond Lake and Countryside Lake and the campus area around the University of Saint Mary of the Lake. Pipes, water tables and housing age differ across those areas, and we plan each visit accordingly.

Our crews also reach Libertyville, Vernon Hills, Lake Zurich, Grayslake and Hawthorn Woods.

What we do not claim

We do not publish invented reviews, counts or ratings. We do not list awards we have not earned. What we offer is plain: a 24/7 line, licensed plumbers and a price before work begins. If you want to check us, ask for the plumber's license details and look them up.

Services

We handle drains, water heaters, sewer lines, leaks, repiping, fixtures, sump pumps, boilers and more. See drain cleaning, water heater repair and leak detection for examples, or browse every service from the services page.

How a call turns into a repair

Every call follows the same path. The dispatcher records your address and the problem, gives immediate steps to limit damage and assigns a plumber. The plumber arrives with common parts, finds the cause and explains your options. Nothing starts until you agree to the price.

If a second visit is needed for a special part, we say so on the first visit and schedule it. We keep notes so the next plumber does not start from zero.

Who we serve

Most of our calls come from homeowners, landlords and small businesses in Mundelein and the neighboring Lake County towns. We work in single-family homes, townhomes, condos and storefronts. Each has different shutoffs, access and rules, and we ask about them on the first call.

If you manage a property, tell us who can approve work and who holds the keys.

Why we answer the phone ourselves

A recorded menu is the last thing you want to hear with water running. Our dispatch line is staffed, and the person who answers can give you the first steps before a plumber is even on the road. That one habit prevents more damage than any tool in the truck.

It is also why we do not run forms. A form can sit in an inbox overnight, and a leak does not wait.

Plain pricing, plain language

We try to explain plumbing the way we would explain it to a neighbor. A cartridge is a cartridge, a lateral is the pipe between your house and the street and a camera inspection is exactly what it sounds like. If you do not understand a line on a quote, ask us, and we will explain it until it makes sense.

You should never feel rushed into a decision. The only thing that should be urgent is stopping the water.

Standards we work to

Our work follows the Illinois Plumbing Code, the state standard administered by the Illinois Department of Public Health (Title 77, Part 890 of the Illinois Administrative Code), together with the manufacturer's instructions for each product we install.
